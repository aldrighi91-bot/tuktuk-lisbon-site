Why families and seniors love the tuk tuk

Many of Lisbon's best sights sit at the top of steep cobbled streets. On foot, that can be tiring for older travelers and small children. The tuk tuk goes up the hills for you, and you step out only at the viewpoints and stops you choose.

The tour is private, so the pace is yours. Need a break, a bathroom stop or a pastel de nata? Just tell your guide.

Good to know for your group

Pickup is flexible anywhere in central Lisbon, including at your hotel door.

Each tuk tuk takes up to 6 people. For larger families, ask us about two tuk tuks or the private van.

Children are welcome as long as they are accompanied by an adult. Tell us their ages when you book.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a lot of walking?

Very little. The tuk tuk drives you up the hills and right to the viewpoints. Every stop is optional.

Can young children join?

Yes. Children are welcome when accompanied by an adult. Let us know their ages when you book.

Can you pick us up at our hotel?

Yes. Pickup is flexible anywhere in central Lisbon, including your hotel.

We are more than 6 people. Can we still go together?

Each tuk tuk takes up to 6 people. For bigger groups we can arrange a second tuk tuk, or the private van for up to 8 people.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel 48 hours or more before the tour for a full refund of the deposit. Between 24 and 48 hours, 50% of the deposit is refunded. Less than 24 hours before, the deposit is not refunded.

Ready to see Lisbon?

Private tours with a local guide. 20% deposit online, the rest on the day.
