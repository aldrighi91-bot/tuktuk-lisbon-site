Why a tuk tuk for your port day

Lisbon's cruise terminal sits on the river, a few minutes from Alfama and the downtown squares. Instead of waiting for a large group bus, your private tuk tuk picks you up and takes you straight up the hills to the viewpoints.

Your guide plans the route around your ship's schedule. Tell us your all-aboard time when you book and we build the tour backwards from it, leaving a comfortable margin to get you back to the terminal.

How it works

1. Book online with a 20% deposit, or message us on WhatsApp with your ship and port date.

2. Share your all-aboard time and how many people are in your group.

3. Your guide meets you at the cruise terminal and the tour starts from there.

4. You are dropped back at the terminal with time to spare.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pick up at the Lisbon cruise terminal?

Yes. We can pick you up at the cruise terminal. Let us know your ship and arrival time when you book.

Which tour is best for a port day?

With half a day or less, the 1h30 Alfama Tour covers the old town and the best viewpoints. With a full day in port, the 4-hour Full City Tour adds downtown and Belém.

How do you make sure we get back to the ship on time?

We plan the route backwards from your all-aboard time and keep a safety margin. Your guide adjusts stops along the way if traffic changes.

How many people fit in one tuk tuk?

Up to 6 people per tuk tuk, and the tour is private for your group.

How does payment work?

A 20% deposit online secures your booking. The rest is paid in person on the day, by card or cash.
